# Registro do conceito visual

Gerado com o recurso integrado imagegen. Uso: exploração de direção artística; não é uma captura do jogo, nem uma spritesheet pronta. A arte exige adaptação manual à resolução, às proporções e à paleta de produção.

## Prompt inicial

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished art-direction concept board for the existing indie side-scrolling game SUPER FEKA GAPS. This is an aspirational mockup, not a production sprite sheet and not a claim of implemented gameplay.
The existing world: a playful platform adventure with Feka, a brown-skinned young adult man with short black hair, black rectangular glasses, royal blue shirt, dark blue trousers and dark shoes; little round red angry enemies; coins, an orange soda powerup, yellow construction helmet, grassy earthen ledges, gaps, violet-green bulky antagonist Joaozao, and underground turquoise crystals.
Layout: landscape editorial game art board on deep ink-purple neutral surround. Two large equally important wide 16:9 gameplay concept panels stacked vertically on the left taking about 65 percent width. Upper panel is sunlit grassy terracotta platform terrain above a visible cave opening, muted turquoise sky, distant misty blue mountains, restrained small pixel clouds and foliage. Lower panel is deep navy/teal cave, irregular stratified rock mass, readable pale stone platform tops, a few dim small background crystals, bright foreground focal crystals, golden coins guiding the route, the same Feka sprite visibly standing on the platform. On right a narrow clear character strip with coherent pixel sprites of Feka, a red minion, and Joaozao; below it a compact set of color swatches.
Art direction: charming, expressive, professionally authored 16-bit inspired low-resolution PIXEL ART. Strict crisp square pixel clusters, restrained dithering only where purposeful, no painterly texture, no blur, no smooth gradient or bloom. Each gameplay panel should look like an enlarged 320x180 game frame, 16-pixel terrain tiles and approximately 16x26-pixel hero. Same pixel density throughout terrain, enemies, items, backgrounds, UI. Readable large forms with a few placed texture clusters, organic joined terrain not a wall of individually outlined bricks. Light from upper left, 1-pixel dark indigo contour on characters, three tones per material, readable eyes and glasses, lively silhouette and running pose with arms and legs. Keep detail modest enough for 320x180 gameplay. Backgrounds low contrast, platforms and actors clear. No huge overscaled hero. Orange soda and gold pickup accents, cool distant scenery, warm soil, blue hero. A simple tiny matching pixel HUD should use one face icon and x3, a coin icon and 12, and a clock icon and 180, each in separate well-spaced areas, never three rectangle lives.
Exact text only: top small heading "SUPER FEKA GAPS"; a small label "CONCEITO VISUAL"; panel labels "SUPERFICIE" and "CAVERNA"; right heading "PERSONAGENS". No other text, no watermark. The typography is crisp pixel lettering. The art and expressiveness should carry the design, with substantial calm breathing room.
Do not copy recognizable licensed game characters or princesses. Preserve the protagonist traits and humorous tone. This is a coherent art concept for discussion, not final exportable game assets.
```
