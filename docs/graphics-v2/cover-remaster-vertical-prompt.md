# Capa vertical · Super Feka Gaps Remaster

Revisão da capa promocional, criada com o **imagegen integrado**, em modo de edição com referência. A direção solicitada é uma capa vertical com cores mais vivas, câmera inclinada, composição assimétrica e ilustração inspirada nas caixas de jogos dos anos 80/90.

Arquivo final: [super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover-vertical.png](../../public/assets/branding/super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover-vertical.png), PNG de **1024 × 1536 px** (2:3).

O título usa **SUPER amarelo**, **FEKA vermelho** e **GAPS azul**, acompanhado de **Remaster** e **new graphics**. A arte preserva os personagens e motivos do jogo; a pintura da capa é ilustrada, enquanto o jogo mantém sua pixel art.

Referência de identidade: [capa horizontal anterior](../../public/assets/branding/super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover.png), preservada no projeto. Feka usa óculos e camiseta azul; Joãozão tem pele verde e roupa roxa; os minions são vermelhos; Yasmin usa vestido roxo e coroa. Esta capa foi preservada como estudo anterior; a composição atual está documentada em [O chão é a armadilha](cover-remaster-abismo-prompt.md).

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: Final promotional FRONT COVER for the original platform game SUPER FEKA GAPS, Remaster edition.

Input image 1 is the previous cover to redesign. Preserve its ORIGINAL CHARACTER IDENTITIES and world motifs, but completely recompose and repaint it. The user dislikes its landscape format, stiff symmetry, subdued colours, all-gold logo, and straight-on camera. This revision must feel unmistakably new.

FORMAT: VERTICAL portrait 2:3, approximately 1024 x 1536. Full-bleed finished flat cover art, no photographed box, mockup, frame, spine, or surrounding desk. All lettering safely inside the canvas.

ART DIRECTION: Beautiful late-1980s / early-1990s Nintendo-era platform-adventure box illustration: confident hand-inked contours, rich hand-painted gouache and airbrush shading, lively expressive cartoon anatomy, jewel-like colour, carefully sculpted highlights and shadows, exceptionally professional illustration. Evoke classic NES/SNES-era printed cover art while featuring only this game's own characters. Smooth illustrated cover painting with a subtle print-paper finish; do NOT reproduce pixel blocks, screenshot staging, modern glossy CGI, or generic mobile-game art. Charming, sunny, adventurous, playful.

COMPOSITION: Strongly ASYMMETRICAL, energetic diagonal action, a three-quarter camera with a modest Dutch angle. The landscape sweeps in perspective from near cropped rock ledges at bottom left toward a distant fortress at upper right. The gap is a dramatic diagonal chasm, not a centred symmetrical valley. Feka is the clear dominant hero, large in the middle/lower foreground, leaping out toward the viewer with naturally foreshortened limbs, one shoe projecting forward and the other leg bent back, lively confident smile and expressive visible eyes. Keep his full important silhouette readable and anatomically coherent, separate from background clutter. His pose should lead the eye across the cover. Joãozão stands on a receding elevated grassy ledge behind him on the RIGHT, leaning into the chase with one reaching arm, smaller in compositional weight than Feka. Place two little red minions at DIFFERENT scales and heights along the LEFT ledges, one near the cropped foreground, one further away. A flowing arc of gold coins and drifting leaves unifies the movement. Stagger focal points rather than mirroring them. Use breathing room, clear shapes, strong depth and deliberate hierarchy. No panels, collage, or repeated protagonists.

CHARACTERS (preserve reference identities):
- Feka: warm medium-brown skin, short thick black hair, large rectangular black-rimmed glasses with clear lenses and visible friendly eyes, short-sleeve cobalt BLUE T-shirt, dark-blue trousers, dark shoes. Young adult cartoon hero, joyful and brave. No cap, moustache, cape, gloves or weapon.
- Joãozão: the game's bulky GREEN ogre, broad square face, short dark-green hair, thick brows, mischievous toothy grin, PURPLE sleeveless outfit and purple trousers, small gold belt buckle, green hands.
- Minions: small squat RED creatures with large white eyes, black pupils, determined eyebrows and little dark feet.
- Yasmin may appear small in the distant right fortress balcony: warm brown skin, long dark hair, plum dress, little golden crown. A secondary discovery, never competing with the hero or title.

WORLD AND LIGHT: Intensely beautiful turquoise/cobalt sky, luminous buttery cloud highlights, lush spring-green grass over warm terracotta and ochre cliffs, indigo-purple distant fortress, teal mountain layers. Hint of an azure crystal cave in a shaded lower rock corner, a waterfall glimpsed far below. Warm sunshine and cool sapphire shadows make forms readable. Saturated, vivid but harmonious primary colours; avoid dusty desaturation and overly busy tiny surface detail.

TYPOGRAPHY AND EXACT TEXT, carefully integrated into the TOP approximately 30% of the cover:
"SUPER"
"FEKA"
"GAPS"
"Remaster"
"new graphics"

The title reads SUPER FEKA GAPS in three compact, playfully offset tiers, with a strong coherent silhouette. SUPER is sunny YELLOW, FEKA is vivid scarlet RED, GAPS is rich electric BLUE. These are their actual dominant letter-face colours, NOT all yellow. Chunky custom hand-drawn cartoon title lettering with ivory keylines and deep navy/purple extruded shadows, slightly rising diagonally, exceptionally legible, spectacular 1990s box-logo energy. Make SUPER smaller than FEKA and GAPS. The logo has intentional irregular offsets rather than perfect centred symmetry and does not cover Feka's face.
"Remaster" is clearly readable beneath the logo on a small tasteful plum ribbon with warm cream lettering.
"new graphics" must be spelled exactly in lowercase, on one small readable vintage promotional sticker in a quieter lower-right corner, not obscuring any character.
No other words, no Nintendo logo, no real console branding, no quality seal, no age-rating badge, no watermark. Preserve precise spelling and all three title colours.
Final result: a memorable, wonderfully composed collectible vertical adventure-game cover with instantly legible branding, expressive character art and genuine classic box-art charm.
```
