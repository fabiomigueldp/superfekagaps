## Prompt de geração

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: finished illustrated cover / horizontal key art for the original indie platform game Super Feka Gaps, Remaster edition. Create one beautiful, meticulously composed landscape image, approximately 16:9, high resolution. Art direction: exceptionally polished authored pixel art reminiscent of high quality 16-bit game cover illustrations. Clearly visible, deliberately placed square pixel clusters and stepped silhouettes; consistent pixel scale, crisp indigo outlines, selective dithering, restrained ramps of 3–5 colours per material. Rich composition and strong character acting, without painterly smudges, vector-smooth edges or 3D rendering. The world should feel inviting, playful, adventurous and recognisably handmade.

Identity and subjects, keep these exact:
- FEKA, the main hero and largest visual focus: a young adult man with warm medium brown skin, short squared black hair, unmistakable thick rectangular black glasses with visible eyes, blue short-sleeved shirt, dark blue trousers and dark shoes. No hat, no moustache, no cape, no weapons. His expression is confident and cheerful. Show his entire body leaping dynamically across a real gap between grassy platforms, one knee raised and one leg trailing, arms naturally balanced. Lively readable silhouette and recognisable glasses at thumbnail size.
- JOÃOZÃO, the villain: much bigger and broader than Feka, a blocky cartoon ogre with visibly GREEN skin on his face AND arms/hands, short dark green blocky hair, strong eyebrows, big toothy grin, purple sleeveless clothing and purple trousers with a small gold belt buckle. He looms on the opposing ledge further back, fists ready, comically menacing rather than horror. Do not make his skin purple, human-coloured or grey.
- One or two small RED minions on a foreground ledge: squat red angular rounded bodies, large white eyes with black pupils, expressive slanted eyebrows, little dark feet, no yellow bodies.

Avoid unnecessary props or clutter.

Composition: elegant poster hierarchy, a strong triangular relationship between the large title, the jumping hero and the boss. Use foreground grass/cliff silhouettes to frame the scene; keep faces and every word away from the outer 6% safe margins.
```
