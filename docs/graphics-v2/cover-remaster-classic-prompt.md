# Capa clássica · Super Feka Gaps Remaster

Nova composição criada com o **imagegen integrado**, a partir do briefing e dos personagens do jogo. Esta revisão responde ao pedido de cores mais sólidas, linguagem das capas dos anos 80/90 e faixas diagonais para os textos da edição.

Arquivo deste estudo: [super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover-classic.png](../../public/assets/branding/super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover-classic.png), PNG de **1024 × 1536 px**, proporção vertical 2:3. Preservado como estudo anterior; a composição atual está documentada em [O chão é a armadilha](cover-remaster-abismo-prompt.md).

## Direção de arte

- Massas de cor mais simples, contornos escuros definidos e sombras simplificadas.
- Título em dois níveis: **SUPER amarelo**, **FEKA vermelho** e **GAPS azul**.
- Faixa diagonal **Remaster** no canto superior direito e faixa correspondente **new graphics** no inferior esquerdo.
- Feka em salto com proporções menos exageradas, plataformas em alturas diferentes e personagens secundários em escalas distintas.
- Fundo creme, céu turquesa, vegetação verde, terra quente, castelo roxo e cristais azuis.

A ilustração é promocional; os gráficos do jogo continuam em pixel art. As versões [horizontal](../../public/assets/branding/super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover.png) e [vertical pintada](../../public/assets/branding/super-feka-gaps-remaster-cover-vertical.png) foram preservadas.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset: One finished VERTICAL 2:3 illustrated front cover for the original platform game SUPER FEKA GAPS. Generate a fresh composition, approximately 1024 x 1536. Flat cover artwork only.

CREATIVE DIRECTION
An exceptionally beautiful, authentically old-school Japanese platform-adventure game package from 1988–1992. Think the graphic clarity and playful cartoon illustration of classic NES and early SNES packaging. ORIGINAL game characters and ORIGINAL lettering, no existing franchise characters or console trademarks.
The style is clean HAND-INKED CEL ART printed in SOLID COLOURS: confident dark ink outlines, carefully designed broad shapes, opaque saturated colour fills, and just ONE crisp darker shadow shape per material. An intentional, controlled limited palette of roughly 12–16 colours. Shapes carry the drawing. Charming illustration and excellent anatomy. It should look like a beautifully art-directed vintage game package, never like a modern noisy fantasy painting or a screenshot.

Absolutely no painterly brush marks, no paper noise, no stippling, no gradients, no airbrush, no glossy bevels, no extreme 3D logo extrusion, no photorealism, no pixel blocks. Avoid the huge boot thrust at camera and oversized stacked logo of generic contemporary adventure covers.

LAYOUT
Use a warm pale-cream SOLID field as the unifying cover background, with a bold, irregular sky-blue scenic shape behind the action, a few clean white clouds, and plentiful breathing room. Art and lettering form a single coherent striking composition. No rectangular inset picture, no collage or panels, no decorative border.
The top 22–25% contains the colourful title. Below it, one lively diagonal action scene takes up most of the cover, strongly ASYMMETRICAL. Camera sees the characters and platform tops in a clear three-quarter view; terrain rises diagonally. A dark-teal gap separates two grassy terracotta platforms at staggered heights. A few simplified distant teal hills and a small purple fortress establish the world. No deep symmetrical valley, no centred waterfall.
The main hero Feka is LARGE and immediately recognisable, around the middle-left, joyfully jumping diagonally toward the upper-right over the gap. His face is clearly visible, his hands expressive, his knees naturally bent, his silhouette open and energetic. Moderate perspective only: proportional rounded shoes, no giant sole dominating the cover. TWO arms and TWO legs, no accessories.
The green boss Joãozão is a supporting threat on a smaller farther ledge at middle-right, reaching toward Feka with a mischievous grin, his silhouette smaller and lower in visual priority than the hero.
One RED minion is a playful, readable secondary focal point near a foreground ledge at lower-right. A short arc of 4–5 GOLD coins gives the composition rhythm. A small grouping of simple cyan crystals in a lower shaded gap references the cave. Use a few stylised tufts of grass, broad flat cliff faces, no repetitive tiny masonry or dense leaf texture. Keep an intentional clean patch at the lower-left for the ribbon. Do not crowd the illustration with extra objects.

GAME CHARACTER DESIGNS
Feka: warm medium-brown skin; short BLACK hair designed as one strong graphic silhouette; thick rectangular BLACK glasses with clear lenses and visible friendly eyes; cobalt BLUE short-sleeve T-shirt; dark BLUE trousers; simple dark shoes. Expressive young adult cartoon hero, confident joyful grin, appealing 1980s/1990s hand-drawn proportions. No hat, moustache, cape, gloves, weapon or logo on shirt.
Joãozão: bulky GREEN ogre, broad square face, short dark-green hair, thick brows and toothy grin, PURPLE sleeveless outfit and purple trousers, small golden belt buckle.
Minion: squat RED bean-like creature, huge white eyes, dark pupils and brows, tiny dark feet. Very simple and expressive.

TITLE DESIGN
Exact title words: "SUPER" "FEKA" "GAPS".
Two-level logo, not three enormous stacked rows: small "SUPER" above the larger line "FEKA GAPS". Playful chunky custom cartoon lettering, with controlled irregularity and a slight upward angle. Beautiful legible letterforms. Give it a dark navy ink outline, narrow cream keyline and a SMALL flat offset shadow, not deep extruded blocks.
SUPER letter faces are solid sunshine YELLOW.
FEKA letter faces are solid bright RED.
GAPS letter faces are solid royal BLUE.
Keep each entire word in its assigned colour. The logo is distinct and compact, occupying no more than roughly a quarter of the cover. Do not put a subtitle under the logo.

CORNER RIBBONS — the other EXACT text:
"Remaster"
"new graphics"
These are TWO DIAGONAL CORNER BANDS, integrated into the graphic design, not signs floating in the landscape and not plaques under the logo.
"Remaster" is printed on a clean straight diagonal dark-navy ribbon crossing the TOP-RIGHT CORNER at 45 degrees. Ribbon ends meet the top and right edges. The text runs along the ribbon, in bold simple cream sans-serif letters. All letters fully visible and well inside the canvas.
"new graphics" is printed exactly in lowercase on a MATCHING straight diagonal dark-navy ribbon crossing the BOTTOM-LEFT CORNER at 45 degrees. Ribbon ends meet the left and bottom edges. Same cream sans-serif lettering and same treatment. All letters fully visible.
The two matching bands have a very thin cream edge and clear flat fills. They must remain small supporting edition labels, not dominate the illustration. Reserve their corners so neither band cuts through a character or the title. No starbursts, badges, wooden signs, scrolls or ornamental frames.

COLOUR AND FINISH
Vibrant opaque yellow, vermilion red, royal/cobalt blue, emerald/lime grass, turquoise sky, plum purple, terracotta orange, deep navy ink, warm cream. Highly controlled contrast, strong figure/background separation, tidy two-tone cel shadows and inviting expressive faces. The whole design reads beautifully at thumbnail size. Print-ready visual crispness, deliberate negative space, polished harmonious retro cartoon draftsmanship.
Only the five requested text strings. No extra branding, ratings, publisher marks, official seals, slogans or watermarks.
```
