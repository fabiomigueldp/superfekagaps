## Behavior and purpose

Replace decorative bird paths with persistent yard actors and integrate aimed flock volleys into the recurring activity director.

## Implementation changes

- HypotheticalAftermathFlock.ts defines YardBird and AftermathFlock. Birds transition through perch, alert, flight, arrive, fall, rest and gone. Staggered departure follows flockFlightPoint(); lead() predicts the same trajectory used by the actor.

- strike() requires a projectile segment to pass within 4.5 native pixels of a flying bird, choosing the earliest eligible contact. A hit switches the existing actor to ballistic fall and emits feathers; it does not spawn a replacement perched actor.

- HypotheticalAftermathScore adds flush-fire as the twelfth action. FLOCK_VOLLEYS supplies three cadences with preparation beats 42/40/44 and total lengths 182/180/190 ticks. The opening flush has its own six-shot list. The director samples target lead through the visible rifle bore and reserves major volley intervals from incidental activity.

- HypotheticalAftermathFlockArt draws two plumages, wing articulation, feathers and fading remains. Existing audio routing gains flutter and soft-contact cues. New flocks arrive from outside the viewport after a quiet interval and only when the visible yard is eligible.
