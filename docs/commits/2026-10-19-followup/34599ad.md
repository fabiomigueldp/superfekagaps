## Behavior and purpose

Replace prescribed aircraft resting angles with support-point contact impulses and add physical interaction with the rear service barrier.

## Implementation changes

- HypotheticalAircraftPhysics.ts extracts AircraftBody, AircraftPose, AIRCRAFT_SHAPES, aircraftPoint(), aircraftOutline() and stepAircraftBody(). Two substeps resolve support vertices using angular contact velocity, normal impulse, friction, restitution and low-energy sleep.

- The main rotor retains its blade phase/rate on release. Its projection changes the drawn outline and contact geometry. A detached tail rotor retains damped accessory motion. Door and panel sheets flatten in depth with matching collision derivatives instead of being forced toward a fixed screen angle.

- After contact, the hull compresses and its nose dents. Art adds exposed edges, cracked glazing, falling-part shadows and bounded contact sparks. Ejection trajectories keep the tail, rotor and cargo door within the directed composition.

- HypotheticalHelicopter adds HELICOPTER_RAIL, a swept skid/barrier check, released rail body, bent supports and merged pavement scars. Marks retain stronger impacts within 32 entries; transient sparks are capped at 28. Helicopter art adds the rear apron, drain and damaged barrier.
