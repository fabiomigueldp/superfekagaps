## Behavior and purpose

Add finite resident discoveries and short pursuits between recurring material activities.

## Implementation changes

- HypotheticalAftermathEscape.ts creates exactly two EscapeEntry residents per yard: rubble and hatch. Each owns its hiding place, exit, stance, Bystander, begunAt/completed state and bounded wall marks. candidate() requires both timing/performance eligibility and a visible source, exit and stance.

- ESCAPE_TIMINGS defines separate peek, run, chase, shot and recovery phases. The actor uses the shared native gait/fall rig. The mount accelerates toward a trailing position and brakes after the resident is hit or escapes; body pose adds recognition, reins and delayed rider compensation.

- HypotheticalAftermath introduces the pursuit phase and fireAtEscape(). Existing scenic projectiles use bystanderIntersection() through intersectShot(); misses can hit the ground or shelter wall. Hit motion retains part of the runner’s velocity and shortens the active passage only after a recovery interval.

- HypotheticalAftermathEscapeArt draws rubble/hatch fixtures, opening occlusion, the emerging resident, dust and retained marks. The director plans the tower before pursuit, and suppresses ordinary incidents while the passage or its approach is reserved.

## Timing, state and scope limits

- Initial eligibility requires at least two completed performances. Starting an entry sets nextAt to tick +1260 and nextPerformance to performed +5. Its begunAt prevents reuse. Rubble/hatch maximum passage lengths are 260/256 ticks before hit-based shortening.

- Each entry retains at most eight wall marks. The removedAt/forage lifecycle is added in 0e96e46; it is not part of this initial escape patch. Pursuit remains background-only and does not replace player control or guard progression.
