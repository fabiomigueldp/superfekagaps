## Behavior and purpose

Add an occasional, occluded ground-scavenging passage for already settled pursuit residents, using planted whole-body motion.

## Implementation changes

- HypotheticalAftermathForage.ts introduces AftermathForage and shares the yard’s existing AftermathEscape entries. Eligibility requires a completed resident fall, no successful escape, no prior removal, at least 360 elapsed ticks since the hit, and visible target/stance. It does not create an additional resident.

- The director chooses planTower(), then planEscape(), then planForage() before the regular score. Entering forage captures current foot anchors and uses aftermathFooting() to plant them sequentially. Motion coordinates support, torso/neck descent, rider lag, tail counterweight, recovery and exhalation.

- Two cadences use a 0- or 44-tick offset. The interrupted version reacts to the existing service chain. AftermathEscape gains removedAt; update() marks removal only after the opaque cover has formed, and EscapeArt excludes removed entries.

- HypotheticalAftermathForageArt adds a low occlusion layer, drifting wisps and dry grains. A district change cancels the old action. Ordinary incidents and flock introductions do not start inside the reserved passage.
