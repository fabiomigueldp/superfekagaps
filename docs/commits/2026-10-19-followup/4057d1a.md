## Behavior and purpose

Connect the helicopter simulation to the visible siege background and existing audio pipeline, and reduce interruptions in the tower sequence.

## Implementation changes

- HypotheticalHelicopterArt.ts adds procedural fuselage, tail, rotor, detached-part, smoke and impact drawing. HypotheticalArt.drawHypotheticalBackground() inserts rear and effects passes; distantRiderPose() consumes the helicopter body and launcher poses. The simulation/timing foundation predates this patch in 9c4c6bd.

- HypotheticalAudio adds rotor and engine-failure synthesis. HypotheticalGame forwards those cues and distant-crash through the existing effects route, and supplies arrival hints while the helicopter interlude is active. These are game-owned voices, not imported sound files.

- HypotheticalTower tracks consecutive unframed steps. A pre-launch abort requires 36 such steps, begin() clears the counter, and retry delay falls from 210 to 48 steps. HypotheticalTowerSearch.ready accepts a sleeping roof body after physics age 135, or proceeds after age 185 even if that body has not slept.

- The aftermath director and backdrop update their framing and pose continuity, and a native authoring renderer is added.
