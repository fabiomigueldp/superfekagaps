## Behavior and purpose

Tie missile ignition to muzzle clearance and stage helicopter destruction around the actual hull contact.

## Implementation changes

- HypotheticalRocketMotion.ts introduces ScenicRocket, rocketTail() and advanceRocketMotor(). Before ignition, motorAge is negative. The helper compares traveled distance with the extending casing plus a two-pixel clearance, returns the fraction of the current step that burns, and raises a one-time ignition transition.

- Both helicopter and tower consume that transition for rocket audio and trail emission. The tower retains its existing speed expression; sharing ignition does not make the two flights identical. The effect renderer clips the emerging projectile at the muzzle, replaces the forward flare with a local gas puff, and renders only the burning path.

- LauncherPose adds independently timed supportReach and triggerReach; launcher art uses them for the hand grips. Helicopter body anticipation precedes the shoulder position, and aim holds through firing before following the wreck.

- The swept hull intersection records point, normal and hull-local coordinates. Breach rendering follows the struck panel, and detached parts inherit attachment-point linear/angular motion. HELICOPTER_BREAKUP separates engine, tail and rotor events at 9, 16 and 28 post-impact ticks.

- Ground dust and marks use the actual support vertex. Hull compression is shared with drawing/contact transforms; explosion lobes, engine fire and smoke follow the new stages.
