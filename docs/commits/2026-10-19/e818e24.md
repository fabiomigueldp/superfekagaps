## Behavior

Improve tower impact readability and stop low-amplitude rubble motion from running indefinitely.

## Implementation

HypotheticalTowerPhysics.integrate() tracks near-support state across two substeps and sleeps a body after 24 frames of sufficiently small total motion. It retains finite-state recovery and reduces lateral roof separation. HypotheticalTowerEffects adds a short expanding impact shape, projectile highlights and drifting exhaust variation. Tower part artwork and the authoring renderer are updated.

## Scope and limitations

Updates tower-cinematic captures and state data, including ignition, breakup and settling views. The sleep threshold is implementation behavior; no new stability or gameplay test is claimed by this retrospective.
