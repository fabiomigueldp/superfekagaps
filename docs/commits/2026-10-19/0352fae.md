## Behavior

Pause an actively playing Classic session when the browser window loses focus or the document becomes hidden. Returning to the tab does not resume play automatically.

## Implementation

The Game constructor subscribes to blur, focus and visibilitychange. handleInterruption() resets Input, lastTime and accumulator on departure and return, and changes PLAYING to PAUSED only on interruption. Adds tests/classic-interruption-pause.browser.mjs. Existing pause/resume controls remain responsible for resuming.
