## Behavior

Keep rocket exhaust attached to the visible motor and distinguish tower fracture materials and contact effects.

## Implementation

rocketTail() shares the motor extension with emission placement and delays the trail until ignition. TowerContact records slide; TowerChip adds stretch and shade; wood becomes a distinct material. Fractures emit at transformed joints, including the bell suspension. HypotheticalTowerPhysics adds clapper response, moving-breach smoke plumes and support-relative shadows; art/effects render material edges and brief local impact light.

## Scope and limitations

Updates the tower authoring set and README. Decorative smoke and light are governed by reduced-motion rendering; the core trajectory and support simulation remain separate from those drawing choices.
