## Behavior

Replace the custom scoreboard overlay with a native modal dialog. Keyboard focus remains inside the dialog, Escape closes it, and closing restores the triggering control or game canvas.

## Implementation

GlobalScoreboard.panel becomes HTMLDialogElement. openPanel() selects autofocus according to form visibility; restoreFocus() handles detached or hidden triggers and queued close events. publish() moves focus before disabling controls or hiding the submission form. Ranking refresh scrolls the focused control into view. scoreboard.css adds native ::backdrop styling, dynamic viewport sizing and focus-visible outlines.
