## Behavior

Extend the tower-search capture from an intact dropped actor to staged damage, simulated fragments and persistent scene marks.

## Implementation

HypotheticalTowerSearch adds thrash, rip, crunch and spit timing points; carried chooses stage/wound/thrash state. New TowerGorePiece, TowerGoreSplinter, TowerBloodStain and TowerBloodSpray arrays track emitted pieces and effects. A seeded generator controls fragments; stepGore() applies gravity, rebound, friction and resting states against rubbleFloor(). HypotheticalTowerSearchArt renders the fragment types, stains and sprays. HypotheticalBystanders exports shared skin palettes.
