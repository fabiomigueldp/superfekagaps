## Behavior

Replace tower collapse pose interpolation with staged, bounded rigid-body motion and separate the effect renderer.

## Implementation

Adds HypotheticalTowerPhysics.ts: TOWER_PARTS, TOWER_RELEASE, TowerBody, TowerContact and HypotheticalTowerPhysics. Parts release in an authored order, then use gravity, angular motion, friction and support contacts. Adds HypotheticalTowerEffects.ts for projectile and impact effects. HypotheticalTower adds accelerating flight, spatially sampled exhaust, a rocket audio cue and contact-triggered rubble/bell sounds.

## Scope and limitations

Tower sounds now follow physical contact events instead of the earlier fixed landing timestamps. The simulation is local scenic physics, not general collision resolution between all moving bodies.
