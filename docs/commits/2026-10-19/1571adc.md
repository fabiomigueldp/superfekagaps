## Behavior

Make tower destruction the first fourth-yard background event and continue it with a persistent rubble search and character discovery.

## Implementation

Adds HypotheticalTowerSearch.ts and HypotheticalTowerSearchArt.ts. TOWER_SEARCH defines grip, yield, roof release, reveal, notice, clamp, bite, drop and completion. The search grabs the actual sleeping roof-left body, carries it at the jaw and returns it to physics. The revealed Bystander uses star-shirt clothing. HypotheticalArt attaches carriedBystander inside the mouth layers; the scene director coordinates tower-search, framing and camera offsets.

## Scope and limitations

At this revision the character falls as an intact articulated sprite. The later staged fragment implementation belongs to 7807bcb. Updates tower-cinematic assets and documentation; directed video is not a full gameplay test.
