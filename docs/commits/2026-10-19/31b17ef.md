## Behavior

Add optional siege side routes and a cargo lift, with district-specific route artwork and post-clear environmental progression.

## Implementation

HypotheticalSiege.ts adds SIEGE_SIDE_ROUTES, SIEGE_CARGO_LIFT, clearTicks and clearedAt. HypotheticalStage replaces one dock support with the lift, adds side-route supports and places corresponding coins. The lift moves between y=196 and y=148 with 650 ms boarding stops. HypotheticalGame adds a cargo-lift hint and passes reduced motion to object drawing. HypotheticalDistrictArt.ts provides district detail rendering.

## Scope and limitations

This commit changes playable mechanism placement, unlike background-only aftermath work. Adds scripts/render_hypothetical_districts.ts and the district-routes-2026-10-19 authoring set. Existing carrier physics supplies lift transport.
