## Behavior

Give environmental incidents distinct actor reactions and render material fragments with contact-to-landing continuity.

## Implementation

HypotheticalAftermathReaction.ts adds reactionDuration(), aftermathReaction() and reactionReplies() for material-specific responses and follow-up action preferences. HypotheticalAftermathParticles.ts adds seeded analytic contactFragment(), fragmentLanding() and drawAftermathContact(). Timber, stone and iron use different trajectories and rendering. The director records actual contact positions, delays the rider response and sends grit sounds at fragment landing.

## Scope and limitations

Adds scripts/render_hypothetical_material_responses.ts plus material-response video, pose images and material-study.json. These paths are analytic scenic effects, not general-purpose runtime debris collision.
