## Behavior

Replace the repeating background playlist with a seeded weighted repertoire and add autonomous environmental incidents and responses.

## Implementation

AftermathRepertoire weights neglected actions, avoids recent repetitions and alternates action families. AFTERMATH_ACTIONS adds shake and breach; aftermathShotPoint() samples a shared aiming path for each shot. New AftermathEnvironment schedules creak, settle, rattle, draft and leak incidents on eligible visible props. HypotheticalAftermath reacts to invitations, coordinates follow-up actions and forwards incident sounds; environment art renders the surviving hardware.

## Scope and limitations
