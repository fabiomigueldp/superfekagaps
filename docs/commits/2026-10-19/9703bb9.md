## Behavior

Add physical prop handling to background searches: timber hauling, stone sifting and hinged iron-shutter manipulation.

## Implementation

HypotheticalAftermathHandling.ts defines AftermathProp, LooseDebris, HeldDebris, handlingTiming(), handlingMotion(), shutterHandle() and stepAftermathProp(). Pieces transfer between pile, jaw and ground; free pieces bounce and settle, while iron uses a driven/released hinge. HypotheticalDebrisArt.ts draws held debris. Aftermath planning aligns actor movement and jaw contact with prop positions; rendering and material audio consume the shared state.

## Scope and limitations

Adds debris-interactions-2026-10-19 images, video and state records. Props use bounded local scenic physics, not a replacement for the player collision engine.
